# Terms & Conditions Specification

## Purpose
The website must have a dedicated Terms & Conditions page and a clear acceptance step in the application process.

## Required application behaviour
The user must:
1. Open/read the Terms & Conditions.
2. Select a service plan.
3. Confirm acceptance.
4. Submit the application.
5. Continue to WhatsApp for further assistance/payment information.

## Acceptance record
Record:
- terms_version
- terms_accepted = true
- terms_accepted_at
- selected_plan

## Content
The final legal wording must be supplied/approved by the website owner or qualified legal adviser.

Do not invent legal guarantees, immigration guarantees, employment guarantees or refund promises.

The UI should make pricing and important conditions easy to find rather than hiding them.

## Required legal links
- Terms & Conditions
- Privacy Notice
- Cookie information where applicable
- Accessibility information where applicable
