# Homepage Specification

## Hero
Use the reference site's hero composition and responsive behaviour.

Suggested primary heading:
**Find opportunities to work with Bupa**

Supporting copy should be short, human and reassuring. Explain that the service helps people explore care-work opportunities in the UK.

Primary CTA:
**Find opportunities**

Secondary CTA where appropriate:
**How it works**

## Search
Create a prominent search component inspired by the reference care-home search.

Fields:
- Job title or keyword
- Location

Button:
**Search opportunities**

Include keyboard support, labels, accessible focus states and useful empty-state messaging.

## Intro section
Explain what working in care can involve and who the service is for.

## Getting started
Use a simple visual sequence:
1. Explore opportunities
2. Tell us about yourself
3. Review the next steps
4. Get support

## Job categories
Use cards for common care roles. Each card should have:
- Role name
- Short natural description
- Relevant image/icon
- Link to explore/apply

## Service information
Explain the service process clearly without overloading the page.

## Plans
Introduce the available service options:
- 3 years — £4,000
- 5 years — £6,000
- Initial payment — £1,000

Make the relationship between the initial payment and selected plan clear in the final approved Terms & Conditions.

## Final CTA
Use a warm, clear CTA:
**Ready to explore your next opportunity?**

Button:
**Apply now**

## Advice content
Include a small article/advice area covering practical care-career topics. Content should feel editorial and useful, not like generic SEO filler.

## Footer
Keep a comprehensive, care-services-style footer with:
- Navigation
- Contact
- Legal
- Privacy
- Terms
- Accessibility
- Social links if later configured
